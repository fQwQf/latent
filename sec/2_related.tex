\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Latent Visual Reasoning Paradigms.} Inspired by continuous thought in language models~\cite{hao2025coconut,shen2025codi,goyal2024pause}, recent MLLMs replace verbose textual rationales~\cite{shao2024visualcot} or pixel-cropping tools~\cite{su2025pixelreasoner,zheng2026deepeyes} with continuous latent states. Autoregressive rollout methods such as Mirage~\cite{yang2026mirage}, Latent Sketchpad~\cite{zhang2025sketchpad}, and LVR~\cite{li2026lvr} feed last-layer hidden states between boundary delimiters and supervise them via MSE reconstruction of helper images or ROI patches, while Monet~\cite{wang2026monet} and related variants~\cite{tong2025skila,wang2026laser,jiang2026univlr,cheng2026hylar,adhikari2026pearl} distill teacher trajectories. To move beyond a single initial span, ILVR~\cite{dong2026ilvr} alternates between explicit textual rationales and momentum-aligned latent segments. Conversely, non-autoregressive bottleneck methods discard hidden-state rollouts: LIVR~\cite{li2026livr} appends $K=16$ static learnable vocabulary tokens in a single forward pass with two-stage attention masking, whereas CVRR~\cite{park2026cvrr} eliminates separate latent slots and recurrently updates intermediate multimodal question representations at a fixed decoder layer. Unlike ILVR (which relies on intermediate text to track multi-hop progress) or LIVR and CVRR (which modify token/layer interfaces for single-turn VQA), we dissect autoregressive latent slots across multi-step relational chains ($K \in \{2, 4\}$) without intermediate text.

\textbf{Mechanistic Diagnostics and Multi-Step Interference.} On single-hop benchmarks, CapImagine~\cite{li2026capimagine} and concurrent probing studies~\cite{viveiros2026holding,zhang2026cosine,zhang2026unsilencing} observe minimal degradation when latent states are perturbed in Monet and Mirage. Beyond Visual Memory~\cite{guo2026beyond} decomposes the latent span into slot contents, boundary markers, and format across six single-hop settings (MVH, ILVR, Monet), proving that slot contents do not act as portable visual memories ($|\text{Normal} - \text{ZeroSlot}| \le 2.7$pp while marker-only decoding $L=0$ recovers $78\%$--$100\%$ of single-hop gains), while carefully noting that this does not imply universal slot inertness and documenting self-looping under corrupted markers (\texttt{ZeroMarker}). Extending this decomposition to multi-step chains, we uncover a critical regime shift: under intact boundary markers (\texttt{Marker-only} $\approx$ \texttt{NoLatent}), uncalibrated non-zero slots turn from single-hop neutrality to severe multi-step destructive interference ($-10.33$ to $-20.00$pp), which is $100\%$ eliminated by \texttt{Zero-Slot}.

\textbf{Evidence Credit and Prefill Bypass Isolation.} To make latent states functional, ReaLVR~\cite{realvr2026} identifies the detached-replay gradient gap in LVR's Stage-2 GRPO and pulls all latent slots toward a single pooled visual prototype $p^+$; however, sharing one static prototype across all slots precludes sequential multi-target tracking ($b_1^* \to \dots \to b_K^*$), and unmasked visual context leaves over half of full-span swaps unflipped. Architecturally, LIVR~\cite{li2026livr} shows that blocking only answer-to-vision attention fails to improve SFT unless prompt-to-vision attention is also blocked in Stage 1, while CVRR~\cite{park2026cvrr} shows that layer-20 question states (\texttt{Multimodal Q}) in frozen \texttt{Qwen2.5-VL-7B} already preserve $99\%$ of visual competence ($75.4\%$ vs.\ $75.9\%$ on V*) and that removing this bypass collapses six existing latent reasoners (including \texttt{LVR-7B}) to $35.6\%$. Our experiments (\texttt{E5} and \texttt{E6}) unify both lines: we prove via 28-layer KV double dissociation why decoding-time vision masking creates a pseudo-bottleneck ($67\%$--$85\%$ masked accuracy with $0.00\%$ slot adoption), and demonstrate that pairing a two-phase image-free interface with step-aligned differentiable sub-windows restores $+63.7$ to $+69.0$pp over shared prototypes and achieves $99.6\%$ sub-window localized logit control.
