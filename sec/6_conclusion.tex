\section{Discussion, Limitations, and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Across over $200,000$ passes on our controlled relational benchmark, our causal audit establishes four bounded conclusions:
(1) \emph{Destructive Slot Interference ($K \in \{1, 2, 4\}$)}: under intact markers, uncalibrated slots degrade single-hop ($K=1$, $-16.00$ to $-21.67$pp, \texttt{E7-2}) and multi-step ($K=2, 4$, $-10.33$ to $-20.00$pp, \texttt{E3-C}) accuracy when distractors place baseline margins near zero, $100\%$ recovered by \texttt{Zero-Slot} (Proposition~\ref{prop:margin});
(2) \emph{Prefill KV Relay}: decoding-masked adaptation (\texttt{E5}) routes visual information via prefill caches $\KV_{Q,M}$ ($96.88\%$--$100.00\%$ eligible flips; $-64.00$ to $-73.00$pp under blind prefill) rather than 28-layer slot caches $\KV_H$ ($0.00\%$ flips, \texttt{E6-C}, Proposition~\ref{prop:bypass});
(3) \emph{Step-Window Control vs.\ Priors}: under LIVR/CVRR-style strict isolation (Proposition~\ref{prop:isolation}), step-aligned windows (\texttt{E6-A}) beat shared prototypes (\texttt{E6-B}) by $+63.67$ to $+69.00$pp on seen colors (via synergistic ROI-MSE and Color-CE, \texttt{E7-3b}) and reach $73.40\%/58.53\%$ full-set flips ($+70.19/+58.06$pp window specificity) on in-vocabulary edits (\texttt{E7-1a}), while $K\times K$ probes quantify $k=r+1$ spillover (max off-diagonal $0.49$--$0.70$ log-odds, \texttt{E7-3a}) and unlocking unseen \texttt{yellow/brown} flips via prior calibration or 8-color+KL trades off $\Vorig$ accuracy (\texttt{E7-1b}); and
(4) \emph{Recurrent Chaining}: on pointer chaining (\texttt{E7-4}), recurrent emission beats parallel emission by $+10.00$pp ($80.67\%$ vs.\ $70.67\%$ on $K=2$; $41.33\%$ vs.\ $31.33\%$ on $K=4$), narrowing isolation loss toward direct vision ($87.33\%/63.00\%$).
