\section{Controlled Multi-Step Benchmark \& Method}
\label{sec:benchmark}

\subsection{123K-Image COCO Census and Frozen Benchmark}
\label{subsec:census}

Testing Propositions~\ref{prop:margin}--\ref{prop:isolation} requires eliminating two shortcuts pervasive in natural VQA datasets~\cite{hudson2019gqa,lin2014coco}: (a) \emph{monochrome repetition}, where target objects share the same color ($46\%$ of GQA dev scenes and $50\%$ of candidates), and (b) \emph{missing same-color distractors}, which allows global perception to bypass relational hops. A programmatic census of all $123,287$ images in COCO~\cite{lin2014coco} (excluding $297$ grayscale photos) revealed that requiring $K=4$ non-overlapping targets with non-degenerate color transitions and $M_{\mathrm{dist}} \ge 2$ disjoint same-color, same-category distractors per target yields \textbf{zero ($0 / 123,287$)} qualifying natural images among $300$ geometric candidates.

We therefore constructed a frozen controlled benchmark from COCO instances with strict train-test separation:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Disjoint Dev and Frozen Eval Splits:} We built $150$ frozen evaluation scene clusters ($92$ instance-composited and $58$ polygon-recolored scenes from $1,070$ source photos) and $50$ disjoint development clusters ($40$ train, $10$ val from $400$ source photos with \textbf{zero source-image overlap}). Every scene enforces $M_{\mathrm{dist}} \ge 2$ same-color distractors and non-degenerate color sequences. Dev-calibrated position permutation matching balances single-hop accuracy across positions $1$--$4$ on the $150$ evaluation clusters within $4.67$pp ($76.67\%$, $80.67\%$, $78.00\%$, $81.33\%$). Each cluster is evaluated across $4$ conditions ($K \in \{2, 4\} \times \{\text{Indep.}, \text{Serial}\}$) and $2$ prompt templates ($1,200$ queries per strategy).
    \item \textbf{Pixel-Level Counterfactual Pairs:} Across $64$ evaluation clusters, we created $87$ pixel-level counterfactual image triplets $(\Vorig, \Vrel, \Virrel)$ expanding to $266$ paired query records ($92$ for $K=2$; $174$ for $K=4$). In $\Vrel$, only the polygonal mask of the edited step-$m$ target object undergoes an HSV hue rotation from $a_m$ to $a_m'$ while preserving luminance and background texture; $\Virrel$ recolors a distractor by the same $\ell_2$ norm; and $\Vrand$ applies an $\ell_2$-matched luminance perturbation.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Two-Phase Full-Isolation Architecture (\texttt{E6-A})}
\label{subsec:method_e6}

All experiments evaluate the official \texttt{LVR-Qwen2.5-VL-7B-SFT} checkpoint~\cite{li2026lvr} and \texttt{Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct}~\cite{bai2025qwen25vl} under greedy decoding ($\text{temperature}=0$). To instantiate Proposition~\ref{prop:isolation} within 24GB VRAM, our **Two-Phase Strict Bottleneck Interface (\texttt{E6-A})** decouples visual writing from image-free answer readout (\cref{fig:teaser}a):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Phase A (Visual-to-Latent Writing via Parallel Differentiable Emission):} Given $[\VT, Q, \Mstart]$, we roll out the $L = 2K$ trajectory $H_{1:L}^{\mathrm{raw}}$ under \texttt{torch.no\_grad()}, and then execute a \emph{single parallel teacher-forced forward pass} on $[\VT, Q, \Mstart, H_{1:L}^{\mathrm{raw}}]$ with gradients enabled to emit the differentiable latent tape $H_{\mathrm{diff}} \in \mathbb{R}^{L \times d}$. Each 2-slot sub-window $H_{\mathrm{diff}}^{(k)} = (h_{2k-1}^{\mathrm{diff}}, h_{2k}^{\mathrm{diff}})$ ($k \in \{1, \dots, K\}$) is supervised with a normalized MSE loss $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ROI}}^{(k)}$ against the mean merged visual token inside target box $b_k^*$ plus a $0.1$-weighted 8-color cross-entropy loss $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{color}}^{(k)}$.
    \item \textbf{Phase B (Strict Image-Free Latent-to-Answer Decoding):} We feed $H_{\mathrm{diff}}$ (without \texttt{.detach()}) into a newly initialized pure-text sequence containing \textbf{zero image tokens and reusing zero Phase-A KV caches}:
    \begin{equation}
      \mathrm{Seq}_B = \big[\, Q,\; \Mstart,\; H_{\mathrm{diff}},\; \Mend,\; Y \,\big].
      \label{eq:seq_b}
    \end{equation}
    Because $\KV_{Q,M}(Q)$ in Phase B is a pure-text cache blind to $\VT$, $H_{\mathrm{diff}}$ is the sole physical conduit of visual information to $Y$, and $\nabla_{H_{\mathrm{diff}}} \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{ans}}$ backpropagates directly into the Phase-A LoRA weights (\texttt{q/k/v/o\_proj}, rank $16$, $\alpha=32$). As a controlled comparison against single-prototype pooling~\cite{realvr2026}, we train an identical model (\texttt{E6-B}) under the same two-phase interface where all $L=2K$ slots share the mean-pooled visual feature of the union of all $K$ target boxes.
\end{enumerate}
