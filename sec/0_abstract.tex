\begin{abstract}
Latent visual reasoning (LVR) aims to replace verbose textual chains of thought in multimodal large language models (MLLMs) by autoregressively generating continuous hidden states between boundary delimiters. While recent single-hop studies show that image-conditioned slot contents do not act as portable visual memories when the image remains accessible and that prompt tokens can leak visual cues, how autoregressive latent slots behave across fine-grained single- and multi-step relational queries, and what trade-offs govern strict visual isolation, remain under-characterized. We construct a controlled relational benchmark ($150$ frozen evaluation scene clusters, $87$ unseen-color and $496$ in-vocabulary counterfactual pairs, and $600$ implicit pointer-chaining queries, totaling over $200,000$ forward and intervention passes) to audit \texttt{LVR-7B} and its adapted variants. First, across both single-hop ($K=1$) and multi-step ($K \in \{2, 4\}$) queries on the same $150$ distractor-rich scenes (\texttt{E3-C}, \texttt{E7-2}), we observe \textbf{destructive slot interference}: while boundary markers alone are neutral ($\pm 0.67$pp vs.\ \texttt{NoLatent}), uncalibrated non-zero latent slots degrade $K=1$ accuracy by $16.00$--$21.67$pp and $K=2, 4$ chain accuracy by $10.33$--$20.00$pp, which is $100\%$ recovered by zeroing slot inputs (\texttt{Zero-Slot}). Second, while single-object recoloring shifts latent states ($\|\Delta H\|_2 = 69.25$) and local visual patch replacement flips $83.93\%$--$93.75\%$ of eligible predictions, donor latent patching yields $0.00\%$ flips and flat logit slopes ($\delta M(1) \in [-0.095, -0.011]$). Third, adapting \texttt{LVR-7B} with step-wise supervision and decoding-time vision masking (\texttt{E5}) raises vision-masked accuracy to $67.00\%$--$85.33\%$, yet 28-layer KV-cache interventions (\texttt{E6-C}) reveal a \textbf{double dissociation}: swapping $\KV_H$ yields $0.00\%$ flips, whereas swapping prefill caches $\KV_{Q,M}$ flips $96.88\%$--$100.00\%$ of eligible pairs and blind prefill drops accuracy by $64.00$--$73.00$pp. Finally, under a two-phase image-free interface (\texttt{E6}, \texttt{E7}), step-aligned 2-slot windows (\texttt{E6-A}) achieve $72.67\%$--$83.33\%$ seen-color chain accuracy ($+63.67$ to $+69.00$pp over shared prototypes \texttt{E6-B}) and $73.40\%/58.53\%$ full-set free flips on in-vocabulary recoloring ($+70.19/+58.06$pp matched-minus-unmatched window specificity), whereas unseen \texttt{yellow/brown} flips require logit-prior calibration or 8-color+KL training that trades off original-image accuracy, cross-step logit probes show non-zero $k=r+1$ spillover (mean max off-diagonal $0.49$--$0.70$ log-odds), and recurrent slot emission beats parallel emission by $+10.00$pp on pointer chaining under strict isolation while trailing same-weight direct vision.
\end{abstract}
