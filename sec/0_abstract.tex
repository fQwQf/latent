\begin{abstract}
Latent visual reasoning (LVR) aims to replace verbose textual chains of thought in multimodal large language models (MLLMs) by autoregressively generating continuous hidden states between boundary delimiters. While recent single-hop diagnostics show that image-conditioned slot contents do not act as portable visual memories when the original image remains accessible, how autoregressive latent slots behave across multi-step relational chains, and what routing mechanisms emerge under visual attention masking, remain unresolved. We construct a shortcut-free multi-step relational benchmark (150 frozen evaluation scene clusters and 87 pixel-level counterfactual recoloring pairs, totaling over 140,000 forward and intervention passes) to audit \texttt{LVR-7B} and its adapted variants. First, we uncover a regime shift from single-hop neutrality to \textbf{multi-step destructive slot interference}: while boundary markers alone are neutral ($\pm 0.67$pp vs.\ \texttt{NoLatent}), retaining uncalibrated non-zero latent slots degrades 2-hop and 4-hop chain accuracy by $10.33$ to $20.00$ percentage points, which is $100\%$ recovered simply by zeroing the slot vectors (\texttt{Zero-Slot}). Second, while single-object recoloring induces substantial latent shifts ($\|\Delta H\|_2 = 69.25$) and local visual token replacement flips $83.93\%$ to $93.75\%$ of predictions, donor latent patching yields $0.00\%$ flip rate and flat logit slopes ($\delta M(1) \in [-0.095, -0.011]$ log-odds), even when target-region visual tokens are locally masked. Third, we expose a \textbf{dual-bypass trap}: adapting \texttt{LVR-7B} with step-wise supervision and decoding-time vision masking eliminates slot interference ($70.00\%$ on $K=4$) and raises vision-masked accuracy from $3\%$ to $67.00\%$--$85.33\%$, yet 28-layer KV-cache interventions reveal a strict \textbf{causal double dissociation} ($0.00\%$ flip when swapping latent caches $\KV_H$ vs.\ $96.88\%$--$100.00\%$ when swapping prefill prompt caches $\KV_{Q,M}$; blinding prefill caches drops accuracy by $64.00$--$73.00$pp). Finally, guided by our exact isolation equivalence theorem, we introduce a two-phase image-free decoding interface with step-aligned differentiable sub-windows (\texttt{E6-A}), achieving $82.00\%$--$83.33\%$ ($K=2$) and $72.67\%$--$75.67\%$ ($K=4$) pure-latent accuracy (surpassing shared-prototype pooling by $+63.67$ to $+69.00$pp) and localizing $99.6\%$--$100.0\%$ of the $+2.75$ to $+3.58$ log-odds counterfactual push within the 2-slot window of the edited step.
\end{abstract}
